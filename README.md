# Ayush Bhadauria / Abix

I work at the intersection of developer infrastructure, proving systems, and blockchain scalability.

Most of my work sits between protocol engineering and developers: validating early SDKs, building reference implementations, improving onboarding paths, writing technical documentation, and turning complex infrastructure into something developers can actually build with.

Over the last few years, I have worked across Polygon's ZK ecosystem, Vana's decentralized AI/data economy, and now ZisK, where I focus on developer experience around zkVMs, proving workflows, and verifiable execution.

---

## Current Focus

I am currently exploring how proving systems become usable developer infrastructure.

That includes:

- zkVM developer workflows and reference implementations
- OP Stack and rollup proving experiments
- SDK/API design for proving systems
- developer tooling for distributed proving pipelines
- verifiable execution receipts for applications, agents, and infrastructure
- privacy-preserving context and policy verification
- technical documentation and onboarding systems for complex protocols

I am especially interested in the gap between what protocol engineers build and what developers need in order to adopt it.

## Research Direction

The direction I keep coming back to is simple:

**How do we make complex infrastructure verifiable, private, and usable without making developers carry all of the complexity?**

Some questions I am currently thinking about:

- How should developers reason about zkVM execution and receipts?
- What does a good SDK for proving systems look like?
- How can rollup proving workflows become observable and debuggable?
- What should the developer experience around distributed proving look like?
- How can private context be verified without exposing the underlying data?
- What kinds of receipts will agents, applications, and infrastructure need?
- How do we design documentation and reference implementations that reduce cognitive overhead without hiding important technical depth?
